// about

One technician. Eight years. A practice rooted in Vermont.

I'm Noah Duncan. Green Mountain Fog is the small consulting practice I run from a quiet part of Vermont — named, honestly, after the view out the window the morning I registered the domain: fog rolling over the Greens.

I spent eight years at Small Dog Electronics, an Apple-authorized reseller that served Vermont for three decades before closing recently. I started on the retail floor and moved into the service department, where I did hands-on hardware repairs and ran phone and in-person helpdesk for everyone from one-person studios to multi-site small businesses.

That experience produced a particular flavor of consultant: comfortable with hardware and software, calm under data-loss pressure, and used to explaining technology in language that doesn't make anyone feel small. I work remotely with clients anywhere in the country, and on-site for the Vermont businesses within driving distance.
